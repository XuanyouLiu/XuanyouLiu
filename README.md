# Xuanyou Liu (Zed)

**HCI researcher** building interactive systems at the intersection of human-AI interaction, wearable computing, and haptic technologies.

[Website](https://xuanyouliu.com) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FcQWVTYAAAAJ) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xuanyouliu/) · [CV](https://xuanyouliu.com/cv-en/Xuanyou_Liu_CV.pdf?v=b9160265f2) · [Email](mailto:xuanyou@u.northwestern.edu)

---

I am a graduate student in Computer Science at Northwestern University ([MU Collective](https://mucollective.northwestern.edu/)), advised by Jessica Hullman. 

I build closed-loop systems that sense human state, decide when and how to act from human-system complementarity, and intervene through haptic feedback. Longer term, I want mixed-initiative on-body interfaces that support continuous, embodied coordination rather than screen-centric interaction.

*Previously:* UChicago ([HC-Integration](https://lab.plopes.org/)) · Northwestern ([SPICE](https://spice-lab.org/)) · UPenn [GRASP (M.S.E.)](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/mse-in-robotics/) · Xi'an Jiaotong (B.E.)

## Research Interests

- **Human-AI interaction:** uncertainty communication and AI-advised decision-making
- **Wearable computing:** novel sensing and unobtrusive input
- **Haptic interfaces:** electrotactile stimulation and sensory augmentation
